Typical turnaround times for common documents like certificates and transcripts.
Processing depends on document type and workload. Typical:
  • Certificate of Enrollment: 2–3 business days
  • Good Moral Certificate: 3–5 business days
  • Transcript of Records: 7–10 business days
  • Diploma / Graduation docs: 10–14 business days
Use your dashboard and Request ID to follow status updates.
Track requests from your dashboard. Statuses and notifications are updated automatically. Use your Request ID when contacting support.
Quick view of common service fees (final amount confirmed by Registrar).

Fees are subject to change; the Registrar confirms the final amount. Typical fees (for reference):

  • Certificate of Enrollment: ₱50
  • Good Moral Certificate: ₱75
  • Certificate of Graduation: ₱100
  • Transcript of Records: ₱150
  • Additional services (rush processing / courier): Variable — contact Registrar

If you need an exact total, please contact the Registrar or check your request details.

Pickup locations, hours, and ID requirements.
Documents are issued at the Registrar's Office (Ground Floor, Admin Building). Bring a valid ID. Authorized representatives must present a signed authorization and ID.
Steps to reset your account password via email.
Click 'Forgot password' on the login screen and follow the emailed link. If you don't receive an email, check spam or contact support.
Contact methods for account help and general queries.
For account issues or general help, email the Registrar or submit a request through the Contact form. Include your Request ID when relevant.
